This policy explains what personal information Damir Hotel collects when you use our website or contact us, how we use it, how long we keep it, and how you may request that we correct or delete it.

1. What data we collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide through our reservation inquiry form or by contacting us directly:

  • Your first and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional, if provided)
  • Requested check-in and check-out dates
  • Number of guests and preferred room type
  • Any message or additional details you include in your inquiry

We do not collect payment card details on this website. No payment is taken at the inquiry stage.

Our website also embeds a Google Maps frame to display our hotel location. When you interact with that map, Google may collect data in accordance with its own privacy policy.

2. How we use your data

The information you provide is used solely to:

  • Respond to your reservation inquiry and confirm availability
  • Communicate with you about the details of your stay
  • Fulfil our service obligations for confirmed bookings

We do not use your data for marketing unless you have separately and explicitly agreed to receive such communications. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes.

3. How long we store your data

  • Inquiry data — retained for up to 12 months from the date of submission, or until your inquiry is resolved, whichever comes first
  • Confirmed booking records — retained for up to 3 years to satisfy legal and accounting obligations under Uzbek law
  • Data is deleted promptly once the applicable retention period has elapsed, unless further retention is required by law

4. How to request access, correction, or deletion

You may ask us at any time to:

  • Confirm whether we hold personal data about you
  • Provide a copy of that data
  • Correct any inaccuracies
  • Delete your data, where we are not required by law to retain it

Send your request to the contact details below. Please write "Privacy Request" in your subject line so we can prioritise it. We will respond within 30 days.

5. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify guests who have active reservation inquiries with us. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
